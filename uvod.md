# Matematične tehnologije

To je spletna knjiga, napisana z uporabo okvira [Jupyter Books](https://jupyterbook.org). Ta knjiga se uporablja za poučevanje predmeta *Matematične Tehnologije* na [Pedagoški fakulteti](https://www.pef.uni-lj.si/) [Univerze v Ljubljani](https://www.uni-lj.si/univerza). Večina gradiva je bila ustvarjena v šolskem letu 2024/2025, v tej obliki pa se prvič predstavlja v šolskem letu 2025/2026.

Predmet obravnava naslednje teme:
- Oblikovanje matematičnih besedil z uporabo LaTeX.
- Ustvarjanje dinamičnih geometrijskih vsebin z uporabo GeoGebra.
- Uporaba SageMath kot simboličnega matematičnega sistema za učitelje matematike.

Cilj knjige je združiti pojasnila, primere in zanimive vaje, da bi študentom pomagali razviti digitalne veščine, potrebne za sodobno poučevanje matematike.

**English:**

This is an online book written using [the Jupyter Books](https://jupyterbook.org) framework. This book is used to teach the class Mathematical Technologies in the [Faculty of Education](https://www.pef.uni-lj.si/en/home-page/) of the [University of Ljubljana](https://www.uni-lj.si/en/university). Most of the material was created during the school year 2024/2025 and it is being presented in this format for the first time in the school year 2025/2026.

The class covers the following topics:

- Design of mathematical texts using LaTeX.
- Creation of dynamical geometrical contents using GeoGebra.
- Use of SageMath as a symbolic math system for math teachers.

The aim of the book is to combine explanations, examples, and interesting exercises to support students in developing the digital skills needed for modern mathematics teaching.
